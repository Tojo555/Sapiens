TEAM Practice: Analyze and Rewrite

The parts of a TEAM paragraph:

T Topic sentence: states the claim and previews the evidence
E Evidence
A Analysis: explains WHY the evidence supports the claim
M Merging: sums up the idea and glides into what’s next
Open the phrase bank (signal phrases and transitions)
Signal phrases (introduce evidence)According to ___, … | In ___, X explains that … | X reports that … | As ___ points out, … | X argues that …
Analysis starters (explain evidence)This matters because … | This shows that … | In other words, … | This suggests that … | What this means is …
Transitions: addAlso, … | In addition, … | Another reason is … | Furthermore, …
Transitions: contrastHowever, … | On the other hand, … | Even so, … | Although …, …
Transitions: cause and resultBecause of this, … | As a result, … | Therefore, …
Merging: sum up + glideSo while …, … | Taken together, … | All of this leads to a bigger question: … | This raises the next issue: …
